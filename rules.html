MEMORY ROYALS

Master Your Memory. Conquer Every Level.

🎯 Objective

Find the required number of King Tiles before your Time or Chances run out. Every level becomes progressively more challenging.

🧩 Tile Guide

King

Collect the required Kings to complete the level.

Bomb

Increases the required King count by +1.

Heart

Grants +1 extra chance.

Joker

Removes one chance.

Clock

Adds +10 seconds.

Fool

Has no effect. It exists only to confuse your memory.

🎮 How to Play

  • Memorize the board before the tiles are hidden.
  • Reveal tiles one by one.
  • Collect the required Kings.
  • Manage your Time and Chances wisely.
  • Avoid Bombs and Jokers.
  • Use Hearts and Clocks to survive longer.

🏆 Win

Collect all required Kings before Time or Chances reach zero.

💀 Lose

• Time reaches 0
• Chances reach 0
